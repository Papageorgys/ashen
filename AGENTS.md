## Design north star

**This is an MMO, party-based game — not a finite ~100-hour single-player
arc. Design for the daily grind and long-tail retention, not a campaign with
an ending.**

What that means for feature and balance decisions:

- **The loop is daily, not marathon.** Optimize for a player who returns most
  days for short sessions over months. Value comes from cadence and
  accumulation, not from a completable content dump.
- **Lean on the time-gated systems that already exist** — daily / weekly /
  monthly contract boards, expedition timers, realm/castle ticks. New content
  should give players a reason to log in *today* and again *tomorrow*.
- **Reward returning, respect the session.** Favor steady, repeatable progress
  (grind that compounds) and catch-up paths over one-shot power spikes. A good
  change makes a 10-minute daily check-in worthwhile.
- **Retention is social and comparative.** The realm ladder, the Hall chat,
  rivals, and the castle are long-tail hooks — persistent, competitive, and
  never "finished." Deepen these over adding finite story.
- **Attachment feeds retention.** Named, marked, bonded champions (see the
  Scars & Deeds / bonds systems) are what a player comes back to protect and
  grow — long-horizon investment, not a one-run payoff.

When a design choice trades "epic finite experience" against "sustainable
daily engagement," choose daily engagement.
